# AI-Powered Invoice Management System
**Project Overview for Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs)**

---

## 1. What Problem Does This Platform Solve?
Many small businesses—such as driving schools, salons, auto garages, and local retail shops—track customer payments and supplier bills using paper exercise books, scattered spreadsheets, WhatsApp photos, and M-Pesa messages. As a result, business owners often struggle to answer two simple questions:
1. **Who owes us money, and how much is still unpaid?**
2. **Which supplier bills do we need to pay, and when are they due?**

This platform brings both sides of a business's cashflow into **one simple, mobile-friendly dashboard**, enhanced with Artificial Intelligence that reads external documents, catches mistakes, and answers financial questions in plain language.

---

## 2. Core Features

### Two Ledgers, One Clear Dashboard
- **Money Coming In (Sales Invoices):** Track what customers or students owe the business, record full or partial installment payments (such as M-Pesa deposits), and immediately see remaining balances.
- **Money Going Out (Purchase Bills):** Record bills received from suppliers, garages, and wholesalers, track due dates, and mark bills as paid once settled.
- **Live Cashflow Summary:** At the top of the dashboard, three summary cards show **Money Owed To Us (Receivable)**, **Cash Collected**, and **Money We Owe (Payable)**, followed by a clean **Recent Ledger** table.

### Live Camera & Document Scanning (AI OCR)
Suppliers send invoices and receipts in different formats—printed paper, WhatsApp photos, or PDFs. Instead of typing everything manually:
- Staff can **upload a PDF/photo** or use the **Live Camera Scanner** (similar to Google Lens) directly from a phone or laptop.
- The AI reads the document, identifies the other party, invoice/receipt number, items, total amount, and due date, and automatically detects whether the document is a **Sales** or **Purchase** record and whether it is already **Paid** (a receipt) or **Unpaid** (an invoice).

### Adjustable Ledger for Any Business Format
Different companies include different details on their receipts and invoices (such as KRA PINs, VAT amounts, vehicle registration numbers, M-Pesa transaction codes, or discounts). Instead of forcing every business into a rigid template, the AI captures these extra details as **adjustable custom fields** that staff can edit, add to, or remove before saving.

### Error & Duplicate Protection
- **Math Verification:** The AI checks whether line items add up to the stated total and alerts staff if there is a mismatch.
- **Duplicate Detection:** Before saving, the system checks existing records to warn if the same invoice number or supplier/amount combination was already recorded.
- **Confirmation Prompts:** Deleting records or marking bills as paid requires a confirmation step to prevent accidental changes.

### AI Financial Assistant & Payment Reminders
- **Floating AI Chat:** Available on every page via a chat button, the assistant answers questions using the business's actual saved records (for example, *"How much does Brian still owe us?"* or *"Do we have any unpaid supplier bills?"*).
- **One-Tap Payment Reminders:** For any unpaid or partially paid customer invoice, staff can generate and copy a polite WhatsApp/SMS payment reminder in seconds.

---

## 3. Typical Workflow
1. **Sign Up or Log In:** A business owner creates an account with their business name (or signs in).
2. **Invoice a Customer:** Create a sales invoice (e.g., KES 15,000 for driving lessons) and record a partial M-Pesa payment (e.g., KES 5,000). The balance automatically updates to KES 10,000.
3. **Scan a Supplier Bill:** Point the phone camera at a garage bill or upload a wholesaler PDF. Review the AI-extracted fields, math check, and any extra tags, then confirm to save.
4. **Ask the AI Assistant:** Open the chat icon to get an instant cashflow summary or check overdue balances.
